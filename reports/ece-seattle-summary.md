# Summary

This report covers <b>30 ECE-related internships</b> in the Seattle metro area (Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Renton, Pinehurst), pulled on 2026-04-18 from Adzuna and JSearch (Google for Jobs aggregator: LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Monster, etc.).

# Compensation

Pay is reported in two formats — hourly (most JSearch listings) and annualized (most Adzuna listings).

<b>Hourly range:</b> $24.00 – $40.00/hr (typical). Median ≈ $28/hr.
<b>Annualized range:</b> $40,000 – $185,000 (Cisco WA range; most cluster $50k–$82k).
<b>Top of market:</b> Amazon Hardware Dev (Satellite) $96.6k–$160k; Cisco Embedded $44k–$185k.
<b>Bottom of market:</b> WSP and P2S/Legence at $24–$25/hr (undergrad).
<b>Special:</b> Waspacegrant offers a $1,400/week stipend.

Many JSearch listings have <b>no salary field</b> populated (8 of 23 JSearch jobs), so the figures above are skewed toward the listings that do publish pay.

# Duration & Working Hours

<b>Standard:</b> 10–12 weeks, full-time, May/June 2026 → August/September 2026.
<b>Hours:</b> 40 hours/week, Monday–Friday, typical 8am–5pm in office.
<b>Variants:</b>
• Amazon Robotics Co-op — 3–6+ months, year-round start dates (winter/spring/summer/fall).
• Kestra Medical — part-time, Spring 2026 → Fall 2026, M–Th in office + Friday WFH.
• WSP — 12-week program starting May 18, 2026.
• Amazon Shenzhen SDE — 4–5 days/week, ≥3 months, June 2026 start, in Shenzhen (not Seattle).

Almost all positions are <b>on-site</b> — no fully remote ECE internships in this batch.

# Graduation Year & Degree Requirements

<b>Most common ask:</b> currently enrolled in an accredited Bachelor's program in Electrical / Computer / Mechanical Engineering, with at least one semester/quarter remaining after the internship.

<b>By role type:</b>
• <b>Electrical/Hardware Design (WSP, P2S, AV, Impinj, OneEnergy):</b> BS in EE/ME, juniors preferred (graduation June 2027 or later for WSP).
• <b>Software/Embedded (Cisco, Disney, Echodyne, Heads Up):</b> Junior or senior in CS / CE / STEM; Disney specifically asks for junior/senior.
• <b>Graduate-level (Blue Origin):</b> Currently enrolled <b>graduate degree program</b> with ≥1 semester remaining.
• <b>Echodyne tiered pay:</b> Junior $29/hr · Senior $32/hr · Graduate (BS complete) $35/hr.

<b>Translation for graduation year (internship in Summer 2026):</b>
• Sophomore-rising-junior: eligible for most listings.
• Junior-rising-senior: <b>sweet spot</b> — eligible for nearly all 30.
• Graduating Spring 2026: <b>ineligible</b> for most (must have study remaining).
• Graduate students: Blue Origin and a handful of others.

# Required & Preferred Skills

<b>Universal:</b> strong communication, teamwork, problem-solving, Microsoft Office.

<b>Hardware/Electrical track:</b>
• AutoCAD, Revit (P2S, OneEnergy, Shimmick)
• Analog circuits, electrical test equipment — multimeters, oscilloscopes (Kestra)
• 3D CAD (Amazon Robotics)
• PCB design, signal/power integrity (Cisco, preferred)

<b>Embedded/Firmware track:</b>
• C / C++ programming (Cisco, Heads Up, Amazon Shenzhen)
• Embedded Linux (Cisco)
• FPGA (Cisco preferred)
• Python scripting / Bash, CI/CD (Cisco)
• Networking protocols (Cisco)

<b>Software/AI track (overlap):</b>
• Python, NumPy/SciPy/Pandas (Echodyne)
• ML frameworks — TensorFlow, PyTorch (Disney)
• LLMs, RAG, langchain (Disney AI)
• Signal processing, time-series (Echodyne)

# Practical Takeaways

• <b>Best fit for most ECE undergrads:</b> WSP, P2S/Legence, Shimmick, Impinj, AV — broad EE skill requirements, predictable summer dates.
• <b>Best pay:</b> Amazon (Hardware Dev / Robotics), Cisco — but more competitive and often citizen-only.
• <b>Best for specialized skills:</b> Echodyne (radar, signal processing), Cisco (embedded, FPGA), Blue Origin (avionics, grad students).
• <b>Apply early:</b> 12 of 30 listings are dated within the past 10 days (week of April 8–17, 2026), suggesting active recruitment cycles for Summer 2026.
